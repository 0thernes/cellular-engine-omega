- **Active Pair (Lafont Interaction Net):** Two computational agents connected principal-port to principal-port; rewriting an active pair depends solely on its two local nodes, guaranteeing strong confluence (Church–Rosser diamond property, `O42`).
- **Bio-Frostbite (`TB14`):** Software interaction-net virtual machine modeling 4-layer linear-ownership biological rewrites (DNA, Organelle, Cell, Creature) with schedule-independent normal forms (`FIFO == LIFO == Random`) and zero-power cryptobiotic port freezing.

- **Feinberg CRNT Deficiency ($\delta = n - l - s$):** Structural topological invariant of a chemical reaction network (`n` complexes, `l` linkage classes, `s` stoichiometric rank); Deficiency-0 weakly reversible networks cannot sustain sustained oscillations (`O11`).

- **Non-Vacuity Witness (`O73`–`O89`):** Explicit concrete Lean inhabitant proving a universal theorem's hypothesis bundle is satisfiable, paired with negative-boundary refutations proving the theorem is not trivially weak.
- **Odd Elasticity ($K_o$, `TB10`):** Antisymmetric elastic tensor component in active solids allowing non-zero work extraction $W = 2 K_o \cdot \text{Area}$ over closed strain cycles (`O32`).

- **TUR (Thermodynamic Uncertainty Relation, `TB08`):** Non-equilibrium bound $\text{Var}(J)/\langle J\rangle^2 \cdot \Sigma \ge 2 k_B$ constraining current precision by entropy production (`O16`, `O17`).
